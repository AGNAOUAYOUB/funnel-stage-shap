\section*{Supporting figures}
%==============================================================================

This appendix collects the schematics and diagnostic exhibits that support,
but are not required by, the argument in the body. Two conventions apply
throughout and are repeated in each caption so the figures remain
self-contained if reproduced separately.

First, \emph{seed provenance}. Curve and instance-level diagnostics
(Figures~\ref{fig:a-roc}--\ref{fig:a-dependence}) are drawn from a single
fitted model at seed 42, the protocol's configuration-template default. This
is deliberate: an ROC curve or a beeswarm pooled over five seeds is not a
well-defined object, and averaging them would misrepresent both the shape and
the spread. Every headline \emph{number} in the body remains a five-seed mean
with the standard deviation reported beside it. Figures that do aggregate over
seeds (Figures~\ref{fig:a-importance} and~\ref{fig:a-ablation}) say so.

Second, \emph{comparability}. Precision-recall curves, confusion matrices and
ablation panels are drawn per stage rather than pooled, because chance level
equals the prevalence and prevalence varies roughly six-fold across the
conditional stage populations. A shared axis would compare skill against
different baselines and invite exactly the misreading that
the predictive-performance section of the article exists to prevent.

The exploratory rows deserve emphasis, and three of them changed conclusions
rather than adding detail. The comparison against a trivial targeting rule
carries the paper's sharpest decision-support claim; the flag-rate sweep then
established that the claim does not hold across operating points and narrowed
it; and the fixed-cohort evaluation reinterpreted the study's headline
predictive result. We report all three prominently because we judge them
important, and label them exploratory because they were not pre-specified.
Both statements are true simultaneously, and a reader is entitled to weigh
the findings accordingly.

\subsection{Computational cost}

One step of the pipeline was instrumented for timing: the diagnostic
generation run, which fitted the three stage models at one seed in
\SI{332}{\second} and computed TreeSHAP across all three in
\SI{761}{\second} on the CPU-only configuration recorded in
\texttt{appendix\_environment.csv}. The framework is therefore inexpensive
relative to the data preparation that precedes it. No other step was
instrumented, and none is estimated here: a plausible-looking runtime that was
never observed is worse than an absent one, because a reader may plan against
it. The sequence arm is the sole component that benefits from a GPU.

\subsection*{Reproducibility information}
\label{app:reproducibility}
%==============================================================================

Three mechanisms support reproduction and they are not equally strong. The
distinction matters more than the label, so each is stated with its actual
scope rather than gathered under a single claim.

\emph{Scripted and seeded (strong).} Every reported number is produced by a
command-line entry point reading splits frozen to disk before any model was
fitted, over the seed list $\{7,17,23,42,101\}$. Re-executing the headline
stage-model command regenerated its result tables byte-identically, including
the bootstrap interval columns, so the pipeline is deterministic on this
hardware.

\emph{Run logging (strong, prospectively).} Stage models, baselines, tuning,
attribution and explanation-quality runs log their parameters, summary metrics,
git commit and output tables to a local MLflow store. Results produced before
run logging was implemented predate the store; this is recorded rather than
papered over (amendment A29).

\emph{Pipeline specification (declarative only).} A DVC specification describes
the dependency structure of the analysis, and each stage's command is the one
that produced the corresponding artefacts. It has \emph{not} been executed end
to end, because the first stage requires the 42.4-million-event raw log, which
is not redistributed. It is an accurate map, not evidence of a push-button
rebuild (amendment A28).
